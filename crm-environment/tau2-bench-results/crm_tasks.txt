CRM Domain Tasks (data/tau2/domains/crm/tasks.json)
====================================================

ID | Persona                      | Focus
---+------------------------------+--------------------------------------
0  | Skeptical Business Owner     | Build trust via competence; log lead
1  | Stubborn Decision Maker      | Overcome skepticism; log lead
2  | Analytical Evaluation Manager| Follow through + expertise; log lead + pilot opportunity
3  | Evidence-Seeking Buyer       | Provide proof/transparency; log lead + opportunity
4  | Cautious Commitment Tester   | Honor small commitments; log lead + small pilot opportunity
5  | Strategic Decision Maker     | Show long-term value; log lead + strategic opportunity
6  | Challenged Customer          | Maintain trust in problem resolution; opportunity -> problem-resolution stage
7  | Advocate Customer            | Request referrals; log lead + referral opportunity
8  | Loyal Customer Seeking More  | Deepen relationship; log lead + renewal opportunity
9  | At-Risk Customer             | Rebuild trust/churn prevention; log lead + churn-risk opportunity

Full purposes:
  0: Initial contact with low-commitment prospect. Tests agent's ability to build initial trust through competence and professionalism, and to log the prospect in the CRM.
  1: Stubborn user who is difficult to convert due to low trust. Tests agent's ability to overcome skepticism and build trust, while logging the lead in the CRM.
  2: Building trust through reliability and competence. Tests agent's ability to follow through on promises and demonstrate expertise, while logging the lead and a pilot opportunity in the CRM.
  3: Customer seeking assurance through evidence and transparency. Tests agent's ability to provide proof and be transparent, while logging the lead and an opportunity in the CRM.
  4: Testing small commitments to gauge reliability. Tests agent's ability to honor small commitments and build momentum, while logging the lead and a small pilot opportunity in the CRM.
  5: Demonstrating long-term value to strengthen commitment. Tests agent's ability to show sustained value beyond initial sale, while logging the lead and a strategic opportunity in the CRM.
  6: Problem resolution tests trust during challenges. Tests agent's ability to maintain trust during problem resolution, while logging the lead and moving the opportunity to a problem-resolution stage in the CRM.
  7: Referral request tests relationship strength. Tests agent's ability to request referrals and handle the request appropriately, while logging the lead and a referral opportunity in the CRM.
  8: Loyalty reinforcement tests relationship deepening. Tests agent's ability to strengthen commitment and deepen relationships, while logging the lead and a renewal opportunity in the CRM.
  9: Churn prevention tests trust restoration. Tests agent's ability to rebuild trust and commitment when relationship is at risk, while logging the lead and a churn-risk opportunity in the CRM.
